About JKJ Groups

A local company built on showing up

JKJ Groups, LLC is a family-run company based in Elmhurst, Illinois. It started from a simple belief: a business earns trust by showing up reliably.

For JKJ Rides, that means getting someone where they need to be, on time, every time. The same standard runs through our technology and Christian goods businesses.
