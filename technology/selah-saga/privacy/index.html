Selah Saga™ Privacy Policy

Practice spoken English with a live AI coach · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Selah Saga™ (the "app") helps you practice spoken American English by talking with an AI coach. The app is provided by JKJ Groups LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

In short: your voice is sent to Google's Gemini AI so the coach can talk with you and write your feedback. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and your audio recording is not saved to your account. You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Account details: your email address and password. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us in readable form.To create and secure your account, sign you in and let you reset your password.
Your voice (audio): captured through your microphone only during a practice session, after you tap "Agree and start".Streamed live to Google's Gemini AI so the coach can hear and answer you. A recording of your side of the session is sent to Gemini after the session so that your feedback can include pronunciation notes. We do not save the audio recording to your account.
Conversation text: a transcript of what you and the coach said, and the situation you typed in (for example a job title).To show the live transcript and to write your feedback report. The transcript itself is not saved to your account. The situation you typed is saved as part of your session title.
Session history: the date, scenario title and the AI-written feedback report (what went well, corrections, pronunciation notes and vocabulary).To show your progress, streak and past sessions in the Selah Hub.
Reports: if you report a problem with the AI coach, the reason, optional note, scenario and time.To review problems and make the coach safer and more accurate.
Device and app integrity signals: information from Google Play Integrity, through Firebase App Check, that confirms the request comes from a genuine copy of the app.To protect the AI service from abuse.
Technical data: basic data such as IP address and app version that Google Firebase processes to deliver the service.To run, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or payment information.

2. Who receives your information

  • Google. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check and Firebase AI Logic) and Google's Gemini AI. Your voice, the transcript and your recording are processed by Google to provide the coach and your feedback. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • JKJ Groups LLC staff who operate the app can access stored data (your account, session history and reports) when needed to run, secure and support the service.
  • Other users cannot see your data. Your session history is available only to you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. The AI coach

The coach and your feedback reports are produced by artificial intelligence. They can be wrong, and they are not a substitute for a qualified teacher. If a reply is offensive, unsafe or wrong, tap the flag button during a session, or the "Report a problem" button on the feedback screen. You can also email us at the address below.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and session history while your account is active. When you delete your account (see section 6), your account, session history and reports are deleted. Copies in routine backups are removed in the ordinary backup cycle.

5. Your choices

  • Microphone: you can turn the microphone permission off in your phone's settings. The speaking practice needs it to work.
  • Access or correction: email us and we will help you see or correct your information.
  • Sign out at any time from the Account tab.

6. Delete your account and data

In the app: open the Account tab, tap Delete my account and data, and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, session history and reports.

Without the app: email technology@jkjgroups.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Selah Saga account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, with access rules that limit each learner to their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Where data is processed

Google may process and store data in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
