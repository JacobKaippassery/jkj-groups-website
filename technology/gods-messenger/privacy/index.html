God's Messenger™ Privacy Policy

Build church with Faith & Action · Last updated: November 10, 2026

God's Messenger™ (the "app") helps people find their parish, see Mass, Confession and Adoration times, follow parish news and events, volunteer, and send prayer or pastoral-care requests. The app is provided by JKJ Groups LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and the app does not use advertising or analytics tools. You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us in readable form.To create and secure your account and sign you in.
Your profile and choices: parishes you save as favorites or follow, groups you join, and your notification setting.To personalize the app and send you news from the parishes you follow. Because this shows which parishes you follow, it may reveal your religious affiliation.
Sign-ups: events you mark "going", volunteer spots, and classes you sign up for. Each sign-up stores your name, the event, the date and the parish.To show counts and help parish leaders plan. Your name on a sign-up can be seen by other signed-in members and by that parish's administrators.
Prayer and pastoral-care requests: the text you write, the parish you choose, and your name only if you choose to share it.To deliver your request to the parish you chose. This may be sensitive, so please share only what you are comfortable sending.
Notification details: a device identifier used by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and the parishes you follow.To deliver push notifications about parish notices and events.
Technical data: basic data such as IP address, device and app version that Google Firebase processes to deliver the service.To run, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or payment information.

2. Who can see your information

  • You can see your own account, profile and requests.
  • Parish administrators can see prayer and pastoral-care requests sent to their parish, and sign-ups for their parish's events and classes. A request is visible only to you and the administrators of the parish you chose.
  • Other signed-in members can see the names on sign-ups for events and classes, so that sign-up counts can be shown.
  • JKJ Groups LLC staff who operate the app can access stored data when needed to run, secure and support the service.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging) to store data and send notifications. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account (see section 5), your account, profile, sign-ups and prayer or pastoral-care requests are deleted. Copies in routine backups are removed in the ordinary backup cycle.

4. Your choices

  • Notifications: turn parish notices and event notifications off in Profile, or in your phone's settings.
  • Parishes: unfollow a parish or remove a favorite at any time.
  • Sign-ups: cancel a sign-up to remove it.
  • Access or correction: email us and we will help you see or correct your information.

5. Delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete my account and data, and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, profile, sign-ups, and prayer and pastoral-care requests.

Without the app: email technology@jkjgroups.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my God's Messenger account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. If you also served as a parish administrator, we will remove that access too.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, with access rules that limit who can read each type of information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Where data is processed

Google Firebase may process and store data in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
